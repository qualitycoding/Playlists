## Goal and scope

Deliver a reliable, honest, secure-by-default Discogs synchroniser (secrets and account data protected; the collection and playlists are public) for Spotify master + six genre playlists and Spotify Recently Added (YouTube Music removed from release 1, D-043); weekly Sunday 03:00 UTC and manual; static read-only GitHub Pages; no paid hosting.

In scope: Python engine/provider adapters, safe OAuth, complete Discogs ingestion, conservative matching, per-target ledger, idempotent writes, guarded Recently Added replacement, encrypted state, Actions workflow, static dashboard, tests, operations and rollback.

Out of scope: public multi-user service, paid hosting, public API server, playlist creation, broad fuzzy matching, YouTube Music in release 1 (D-043), live writes/deploy during planning, history rewrite without G-005.

## Success criteria

1. Fetch every Discogs page or fail closed.
2. Per-target completion only after verified provider write/read-back.
3. Reruns and overlapping runs create no duplicates.
4. Master/genre playlists remain add-only; Recently Added alone is replaced under the D-033 rules.
5. Ambiguous matches remain unmatched.
6. Dry-run performs zero writes and no durable-state mutation (stated exception, D-047: an Actions dry-run persists a rotated Spotify token through the commit job).
7. Pages, logs, artefacts and gate evidence cannot fake sync or expose secrets or account data.
8. State is encrypted or stored outside the public repository, with a key lifecycle (D-035).
9. Workflow uses least privilege and fails visibly.
10. All 72 frozen tests except those marked deferred for YouTube Music (D-043) and the security scans pass; Pages smoke/rollback pass.
11. Owner approves adapter, migration, history and live writes at the gates. Approval is recorded with its channel in the GATE file; it is procedural, not enforced, while R-043 stands.
12. Matching precision and recall are reported against a labelled fixture with an owner-agreed floor.
13. A stale status is visible (STALE after 8 days).
